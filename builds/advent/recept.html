Gingerbread Men

Bake your own gingerbread men for Christmas!

🍪 about 20 cookies⏰ about 1.5 hours🧑‍🍳 with a grown-up

🧺 You need

✨ For the icing: 100 g icing sugar and 1–2 teaspoons of lemon juice. Add the juice slowly — the icing must be thick.

👩‍🍳 How to make them

Enjoy your cookies with a glass of milk! Merry Christmas!
