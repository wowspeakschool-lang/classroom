```
This is a story about three people who travel a lot: Marco, Clara and Danny.
Marco is a travel blogger. He writes about the places he visits. Clara is a tour
guide in the mountains, and her brother Danny is a photographer. Their mother is
a teacher, and she only travels in the summer.
Marco says New Zealand is the most beautiful country he knows. Clara says Norway
is the most expensive country she knows, and Danny says Iceland is the most
dangerous country for photographers, because of the weather. New Zealand isn't
the biggest country — Marco says it is quite small.
Marco thinks people in New Zealand are friendlier than people in other countries.
Clara thinks they are funnier, and Danny thinks they are cleverer. Nobody says
they are richer.
All three of them know the Amazon forest well. It is dangerous there, but not
because the mountains are very high and not because it is too cold — there are
dangerous animals in the forest, and the people there are very friendly.
```

Вопросы блока остаются как есть:

1. What is Marco's job? → **travel blogger** · tour guide · photographer · teacher
2. What does Marco think about New Zealand? → **It's the most beautiful country he knows** ·
   the biggest · the most expensive · the most dangerous
3. People in New Zealand are … than in other countries → **friendlier** · funnier · cleverer · richer
4. Why is the Amazon forest dangerous? → **There are dangerous animals** ·
   The mountains are very high · It's too cold there · People aren't friendly
